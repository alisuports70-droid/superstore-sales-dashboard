# Superstore Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Tools Used
Power BI, CSV data

## What I Analyzed
- Sales performance across 3 categories: Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies
- Year over year sales trend from 2015 to 2018
- Sales distribution across USA states
- Regional filtering (East, West, Central, South)

## Key Insights
- Technology is the highest selling category at $836K total
- Sales grew consistently every year, nearly doubling from 2015 to 2018
- Eastern region drives the most sales volume
